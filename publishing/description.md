Bring your soup into your Create kitchen. Empty a bowl on an Item Drain, store the soup in tanks, and use a Spout to serve it again.

{{draining}}

Each bowl holds **250 mB**, so one bucket makes **four bowls**. You get the empty bowl back when draining, and each soup stays a separate fluid with its own animated texture and bucket.

{{filling}}

### What's on the menu?

All four Minecraft soups: mushroom stew, beetroot soup, rabbit stew and suspicious stew.

With **Farmer's Delight**, you can also use beef stew, chicken soup, vegetable soup, fish stew, pumpkin soup, baked cod stew, noodle soup, onion soup and bone broth. Only dishes available in your installed version are enabled.

Suspicious stew keeps its effects when moved between bowls, buckets and tanks. Different effects stay separate. Keep it in containers: pouring it onto the ground loses those effects.

{{kitchen}}

Find the buckets in creative search or **Tools & Utilities**. Names are available in English and Spanish (Spain), and the fluids work with Create's tank displays and Jade.

### Installation

Made for **Minecraft 1.21.1**, **NeoForge 21.1.251** and **Create 6.0.10 for NeoForge**. Install the addon on both the client and server. Farmer's Delight is optional; version 1.3.4 was used for the full soup menu shown here.

The screenshots were taken in Minecraft, with the HUD hidden, while the machines were running.
